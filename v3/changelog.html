Crawlers v3 Changelog

Every release, newest first: what changed and why. Play the game

v3.6.3

2026-11-08 20:49 EST

Back to three crisp zoom steps, and the crawlers are tethered to the wagon beast so the camera seldom has to switch.

  • The smooth zoom and the hard-edged change of v3.6.2 are undone: the picture looks as it did before them.
  • The camera uses three crisp steps again: game pixels of 3, 2 or 1 screen pixels, closing in again only once everyone has been together a couple of seconds.
  • In a fight, a crawler only goes after creatures within 4 squares of the wagon beast (plus its own reach). Anything further out comes to them or is left, and a crawler with nothing in range goes back beside the beast.
  • Two minutes at ×4 speed: the camera switched step twice, across 11 floors.

v3.6.2

2026-11-08 20:43 EST

No more smoothing: pixels stay hard-edged at every zoom while the camera glides.

  • Textures always show whole texture pixels, never a blend of neighbouring ones or of their finer and coarser versions.
  • The picture's last step takes one sample per pixel instead of averaging four, so edges stay sharp.
  • Between the zooms where the textures fit exactly, neighbouring pixels can differ by a screen pixel in size and shimmer slightly as the view moves (your choice: "Glide, hard-edged pixels").

v3.6.1

2026-11-08 18:58 EST

Fixed: opening Town while the Cart was open left the game stopped for good. Neither stops the game any more.

  • The cart and Town no longer pause the game: you pack and shop while the party goes on. That also ends the freeze, which came from the two pausing and un-pausing in the wrong order.
  • Loot that lands in the cart while you are holding a piece is drawn once you put the piece down.
  • Tapping something you can't afford in Town now says so ("Not enough gold: that costs 40, and 13 is banked") with a little shake, instead of doing nothing.

v3.6.0

2026-11-08 15:02 EST

The interface half never scrolls, the crawlers sit as thirds of one line, and the fork is a pixel signpost over the dungeon.

  • The title and version now sit in a corner over the dungeon.
  • The fork is a small wooden signpost over the dungeon: two arrow planks pointing up-left and up-right, each with a pixel picture (crossed swords, a skull, a chest, a campfire) and the name of what lies past it. A fuse burns down for the ten seconds. Tap a plank to turn.
  • The interface half fits without scrolling, in landscape or portrait: a strip with the floor, gold and buttons, then the three crawlers side by side as thirds of the line, then one space for the log or the open panel.
  • The skill map, the cart's grid and the upgrade and town cards shrink to fit that space. Opening one closes the others.

v3.5.0

2026-11-08 14:50 EST

The screen is split in half: the dungeon in one, everything you read and tap in the other, in landscape or portrait.

  • Landscape: the dungeon on the left, the interface on the right. Portrait: the dungeon above, the interface below. Turning the device switches between them.
  • The interface half holds the floor and gold, the buttons, the fork, the upgrade and town cards, the cart's packing grid, the skill maps, the party's cards and the log. It scrolls when there is more than fits.
  • Damage numbers, coins and shots still fly over the dungeon itself.
  • The dungeon's picture now follows the window when it is resized or turned.

v3.4.1

2026-11-08 13:04 EST

Fixed: two crawlers could stand on one square, one hidden inside the other. The wagon beast now blocks the way, and crawlers go round each other.

  • No one was missing: the Finesse and Magic crawlers often stopped on the same square. Now no one stops where somebody already stands; if it happens, one steps aside.
  • Walking through a friend now costs a crawler as much as going 8 squares out of its way, so it goes round when there is room; where there isn't, it waits a moment for them to move before squeezing past.
  • Nobody walks through the wagon beast. A crawler standing where it needs to go steps aside, and it waits until they have.

v3.4.0

2026-11-08 11:44 EST

The run never stops: the labyrinth unfolds up the screen with forks to choose at, gold banks itself, and the wagon beast is now a big biomechanical crab.

  • The way on runs up the screen. Each new piece of the labyrinth is the next floor, and the party never stops between them.
  • At a fork, a pop-up shows the two ways (up-left and up-right) and what lies past each, for 10 seconds. The party is already heading one way, picked at random; tap the other to turn.
  • Gold is banked the moment it drops. Cashing out is gone; falling costs only depth (3 floors).
  • Each floor banks an upgrade pick and a skill point for each crawler. Spend them any time from Upgrades and the crawler cards; the run keeps going.
  • Town opens any time from its button: train, buy gear, sell the cart's valuables and spare gear. The game waits while it is open.
  • The wagon beast is now a crab of chitin and bone: a wide shell, eyes on stalks, two pincers, ribbed tubes, glowing spots, and vents that breathe.
  • It is larger and stands much higher, never lower than clears the ground under its whole shell, so it no longer sinks into stairs. Its steps are twice as close together and quicker.
  • Halls are 3 squares wide, so at least 2 stay clear. Crawlers wait behind or step past one another, never going round backwards.
  • The camera now glides in and out to keep everyone in view, with the pixels kept the same crisp size, in place of three fixed steps.

v3.3.0

2026-11-08 11:09 EST

The mule and cart are now one creature: a six-legged, steam-and-magic wagon beast with the wagon's bed on its back.

  • It fits in one square: a brass and copper body with glowing lens eyes, a glowing core and runes beneath, a horn with a crystal, and a chimney that puffs steam.
  • The wagon's bed rides on its back, with the loot in it where you packed it, still jostling a few inches as it walks.
  • It walks on six jointed legs, three at a time while the other three hold it up. Each foot is set down on the ground itself, on a slope's face, a step's tread or a half block's top, and lifted clear of a step up.
  • Its body rides on its feet and leans with the ground under them. Turning a corner, no foot reaches into a wall.
  • Click it (or the Cart button) to pack the bed, as before.

v3.2.3

2026-11-08 10:10 EST

No shooting through walls, loot that jostles instead of bouncing, and the game at half its old pace.

  • Shots, spells and heals need a clear line to their target. A crawler with no clear line walks closer until it has one.
  • Loot in the cart now jostles a few inches as the wheels roll, no higher.
  • ×1 now runs the game at half the speed it did; ×2 and ×4 are half as fast too. The choice cards wait twice as long.

v3.2.2

2026-11-08 10:01 EST

The camera keeps the whole party in view.

  • It centres on everyone together, the crawlers, the creatures they call up, and the mule and cart, rather than on one crawler.
  • When they spread out it draws back until all of them fit; once they are together again for a moment it closes back in.

v3.2.1

2026-11-05 13:05 EST

Fixed: the party could stop for good on the way to a door, waiting for a mule that could not reach them.

  • The mule now walks exactly where the party's leader walked, so it follows them up and down stairs too.
  • If it is still stuck for a while, it finds its own way and catches up.
  • A floor with no door out no longer stops the game: more creatures come to the party, and it counts as the next floor.
  • The party, the creatures they call up and the mule step past one another: in a narrow way, a crawler standing in front of the leader held everyone up for good.

v3.2.0

2026-11-05 11:58 EST

A mule pulls a tilted loot cart, the loot rides in it, and packing the cart is a shape game. The skill trees are now maps of nodes.

  • The party escorts a mule pulling a two-wheeled cart that leans back. Out of a fight they wait for it. Creatures leave it alone.
  • Loot flies into the cart and rides there as real objects, bouncing and tipping as the wheels roll.
  • Click the cart (or the Cart button) and it zooms into a grid. Drag things to pack them, turn them with R, a right-click, or the Turn button.
  • Valuables are sold when you cash out. Charms help the whole party while they're packed. Spare gear rides here too (the bag is gone).
  • Things are worth more beside what they want: gems beside gems, a whetstone beside a weapon. A ★ marks each match.
  • What has no room lies on the ground beside the cart, and is left behind when the party takes a door. The game waits while you pack.
  • Each of the 9 skill trees is now an uneven map of 20 nodes: small steps between the named skills, and a crown at the top.
  • Tap a node to read it, double-tap or press "Spend a point" to take it. A node opens once a node it hangs from has a point.
  • Points spent before this version come back to be spent again.

v3.1.0

2026-11-05 11:31 EST

Three classes with three skill trees each, six gear slots, and a master sheet for every number.

  • The party is one Might (up front), one Finesse (throws from a distance) and one Magic (casts, heals, calls up creatures).
  • Each class has 3 skill trees of 5 skills: Guardian, Berserker, Warlord; Marksman, Shadow, Trickster; Elementalist, Light, Summoner.
  • Finishing a floor gives every crawler a skill point. Tap a crawler's card to open their trees and spend points whenever you like; a tree's next row opens after 2 points in it.
  • Gear is now 6 slots: head, body, hands, feet, main hand and off hand. Some gear is for one class only; the bag only offers it to who can wear it.
  • The lamps and torches the Finesse and Magic carry in the off hand light the way; Might carries a shield.
  • New master sheet: every number and name of the rules, read by the game when it is built. The old run starts fresh.

v3.0.0

2026-11-05 10:25 EST

Crawlers v3 begins: an arcade idle dungeon crawler on Crawlers v2's look.

  • A party of three (a Fighter, an Archer and a Healer) fights its way through v2's labyrinth on its own, one floor at a time.
  • Every blow throws up a number, creatures burst into coins that fly to the gold counter, and quick kills build a combo.
  • Between floors: pick 1 of 3 upgrades, then pick the next door, each saying what lies past it (creatures, brutes, treasure or rest).
  • Gear drops from creatures: better pieces go straight on whoever they help most, and the rest wait in the bag to be handed out.
  • Cash out at any door: go up to town, bank the gold, train or buy, and come back down. Pushing on makes the gold found grow.
  • If the whole party falls, they wake 3 floors up with half the gold they carried.
  • A choice left alone is made for you when its bar runs out. Speed ×1, ×2 or ×4. The run is kept between visits.
